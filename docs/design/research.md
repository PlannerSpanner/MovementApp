## 1. Ravi Zupa: qualities to take

**Gap first.** I could not view any artwork image. Everything here comes from gallery listings, biographies and interviews. I also found no series named "Tao Te Ching". I found two 2019 screenprints that use Tao Te Ching chapters as text; one is listed in his "Tattoo Gratitude Series". https://thearthoundgallery.com/products/ravi-zupa-stay-at-the-centre [read]

- **Inks.** Few. One of the two is a 2-colour screenprint (same page [read]). The other is 5-colour. https://thearthoundgallery.com/products/ravi-zupa-it-is-inexhaustible [snippet; HTTP 429] His screenprints run two to five colours. https://blackbookgallery.com/artists/ravi-zupa [read] No source names the hues.
- **Paper.** Heavy uncoated stock: "100# French paper (Construction Line)". https://thearthoundgallery.com/products/ravi-zupa-stay-at-the-centre [read] Other prints are on 80 lb Cream French paper. https://www.outregallery.com/collections/ravi-zupa [snippet; HTTP 429]
- **Hand-made.** "Zupa does not create any of his art digitally; everything comes from his own hand." https://www.harmanprojects.com/artists/165 [read]
- **Processes.** Screen print and block print. One image may exist as "a block print and a drawing and a screen print and a painting". https://hypocritereader.com/96/interview-zupa [read] Some pieces mix a printed figure with hand-drawn surroundings or metal leaf. https://blackbookgallery.com/artists/ravi-zupa [read] He has described carving one block per colour, then pulling one screen per colour for larger posters. https://news.1xrun.com/ravi-zupa-makes-1xrun-debut-sante-muerte-prints-hand-painted-multiples/ [snippet; would not resolve]
- **Lettering.** Text is a full partner of the image. On one print a chapter sits inside the composition and another "wraps around the perimeter of the piece". https://thearthoundgallery.com/products/ravi-zupa-stay-at-the-centre [read] He calls text "the most time-consuming part of the process". https://hypocritereader.com/96/interview-zupa [read]
- **Influences.** "German Renaissance printmakers, Flemish primitives, abstract expressionists, Japanese woodblock artists, and Mughal painters" (gallery wording). https://www.harmanprojects.com/artists/165 [read] In his words: "Japanese printmaking, Flemish paintings, a lot of Hindu art". https://hypocritereader.com/96/interview-zupa [read] Another bio adds "vintage print, Renaissance woodcuts". https://thearthoundgallery.com/products/ravi-zupa-stay-at-the-centre [read]
- **Not confirmed:** hatching, line weight, caps versus lower case, banners, borders, keylines, seals or stamps, texture, misregistration. Someone must look at the prints to brief these.

**Brief from what is confirmed:** two to five flat inks on warm uncoated paper; every mark hand-drawn; one layer per colour; text treated as image and allowed to frame the picture.

**Do not take:** his subjects, figures or emblems; his compositions or text layouts; his letterforms; any artwork, crop or redrawing; his titles or his pairing of a chapter with an image. Take the method only. All drawing and lettering must be new.

## 2. Interaction tone

### Gentler Streak

- "Rest days keep your streak alive." It suggests "a walk, a workout, or a well-earned rest". https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gentler-streak-workout-tracker/id1576857102 [read]
- You can set a status of sick, injured or on a break; the app "shifts its focus from activity to wellbeing". Same page [read]
- "No scores to chase, no rings to close - just plain words". Same page [read]
- Copy rule: "supportive but not cheesy, motivating but not fake-hyped". Mood: "soft, cozy, warm, homey atmosphere". https://sketch.com/blog/gentler-streak [read]
- A mascot illustration, Yorhart, tops the main tab with the day's status: "a love letter from your heart". Same page [read]
- Main screen: today's plan, one bar with a green target zone, then recommendations. https://www.tapsmart.com/features/deep-dive-gentler-streak/ [read]
- **Not confirmed:** haptics, sound, specific animations.

**What it does that a plain interval timer does not:** it counts rest as success and speaks in kind, plain sentences.

### (Not Boring) Habits

- "We know things happen and days will be missed." "Pick up right where you left off". https://notbor.ing/product/habits [read]
- Progress is a journey: eight levels over 60 days, each with "words of support". Same page [read] Apple's article says 66 levels; the sources disagree. https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=9ab1g4r3 [read]
- Check-in is one gesture. Hold anywhere on screen. A wind-up animation builds. Release early and it reverses. Finish and a full-screen starburst fires. https://notbor.ing/words/the-most-satisfying-checkbox [read]
- Sound swells, then releases in a "ka-thunk". The haptic pattern was made from that sound, and plays even when muted. Same page [read]
- Look: monochrome with flashes of colour; each day adds to a 3D trophy. https://www.tapsmart.com/apps/review-not-boring-habits/ [read]
- "The best rewards are often not the grandest but the most symbolic." https://notbor.ing/words/the-most-satisfying-checkbox [read]

**What it does that a plain interval timer does not:** it makes finishing feel physical (wind-up, sound, haptic) and never punishes a gap.

## 3. Session structure

### Down Dog

- **During:** instructor video in a clean player; other elements fade. https://screensdesign.com/showcase/yoga-down-dog [read] A strip of pose thumbnails sits above the progress bar; tap one to jump. Subtitles optional. https://www.downdogapp.com/support [read]
- **Transitions:** spoken instruction, set from Full to Less. Transition Speed and Hold Lengths sliders. Same page [read] **Not confirmed:** chime, on-screen countdown, pose-name label.
- **End:** a post-practice page to favourite or share; challenge options appear. Same page [read] **Not confirmed:** any celebration.
- **History:** dated Practice History; a Dashboard with weekly streak, a month calendar of practised days, and Milestones. Same page [read]

**What it does that a plain interval timer does not:** it shows the whole session as a tappable strip, and lets you choose how much the voice talks.

### pliability (formerly ROMWOD)

- **During:** video-led, spa-like music, poses held for minutes. https://athletechnews.com/product-of-the-week-pliability-stretching-app-review/ [read] **Not confirmed:** on-screen timer or next-up.
- **Transitions:** a calm voice; a user says it sometimes announces 30 seconds left. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pliability-mobility-recovery/id1175346453 [read, user reviews] One reviewer found the voice unclear and watched the video instead. https://athletechnews.com/product-of-the-week-pliability-stretching-app-review/ [read]
- **End:** **Not confirmed.**
- **History:** home shows streaks; the week fills with dots. https://pluggedingolf.com/pliability-app-review/ [read]

**What it does that a plain interval timer does not:** long, unhurried holds under a calm voice, and a week of dots that fills in.

### Apple Fitness+

- **During:** trainer video; time remaining, heart rate and calories on the left, Activity rings on the right; each can be hidden. https://reviewed.com/health/content/apple-fitness-app-review [read] Interval timers still show when the main time is off. https://support.apple.com/en-us/111096 [read]
- **Transitions:** the trainer's voice; two more trainers show easier and harder versions. https://reviewed.com/health/content/apple-fitness-app-review [read] Optional Audio Hints; Trainer or Music Focus. https://support.apple.com/en-us/111096 [read] **Not confirmed:** countdown sounds.
- **End:** trainer signs off with "close your rings"; a summary shows on screen and Watch. https://reviewed.com/health/content/apple-fitness-app-review [read]
- **History:** awards, with workouts suggested "to maintain your streak". https://support.apple.com/guide/fitness-plus/dev4b830ebf6/ios [read]

**What it does that a plain interval timer does not:** a human leads every change, and every number can be switched off.
